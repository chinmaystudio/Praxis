Flagship Technical Symposium

Bridging Pure Research With Actionable Technology

Organized by the Department of Information Technology & ITSA at PCCOE Pune, ResearchX is a national-level technical competition tailored for undergraduate engineering students. The event guides teams from fundamental aptitude and strategic AI auctions to defending original, technology-driven solutions for global resource conservation before an expert evaluation panel.

01

Team Composition

Each team must consist of 2 to 4 registered students. Solo entries and 5+ member teams are strictly not permitted. Interdisciplinary squads are welcomed.

02

Academic Integrity

Plagiarism, fabricated data, or presenting uncredited AI content as original will result in immediate disqualification. Play fair, research honestly, present with integrity.

03

Active Participation

All registered members must be present and actively participate throughout each qualified round and jury Q&A sessions.

04

Coordinators & Jury Authority

The decisions of the organizing student coordinators and faculty judging panel are final and binding in all technical and procedural matters.
